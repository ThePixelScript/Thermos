## 1. The Five-Tier Provenance Classification Framework

Urban climate planning merges heterogeneous datasets spanning multi-spectral satellite imagery, cadastral GIS layers, decennial census tables, and empirical simulation models. Treating these disparate sources as equally "factual" introduces false scientific confidence and municipal liability.

THERMOS implements a strict five-tier data classification enum ([backend/app/schemas/common.py](../backend/app/schemas/common.py)):

```python
class DataClassification(str, Enum):
    OBSERVED = "OBSERVED"    # Direct physical measurement
    DERIVED = "DERIVED"      # Mathematical transformation of observed data
    ESTIMATED = "ESTIMATED"  # Statistical or downscaled proxy
    SIMULATED = "SIMULATED"  # Forward counterfactual model output
    ASSUMED = "ASSUMED"      # Policy constant or engineering assumption
```

### Classification Definitions:
1. **`OBSERVED`:** Directly captured by physical remote-sensing instruments or verified ground surveys.
2. **`DERIVED`:** Directly computed from observed measurements via closed-form deterministic mathematics without empirical curve-fitting or statistical extrapolation.
3. **`ESTIMATED`:** Inferred through statistical regressions, census tract downscaling, or proxy indicators where direct parcel measurements are unavailable.
4. **`SIMULATED`:** Counterfactual forward projections evaluated under hypothetical planning interventions. Subject to physical assumptions and damping models.
5. **`ASSUMED`:** Configurable planning constants, engineering unit prices, and policy parameters.

## 3. Synthetic vs Real Data Audit

The Phase 1 MVP operates on pre-validated, calibrated synthetic urban zones ([data/processed/sample_zones.json](../data/processed/sample_zones.json)) designed to represent diverse urban typologies.

### Transparent Audit Rules:
* **No Invented Municipal Entities:** Synthetic datasets are explicitly identified as demo models. The platform never invents fictitious municipal health registries or fraudulent survey citations.
* **Evidence Labeling:** Evidence objects generated by [risk_engine.py](../backend/app/modules/risk/risk_engine.py) include explicit provenance tags:
  - `LANDSAT_LST_DEMO`: Calibrated thermal infrared proxy.
  - `SYNTHETIC_MUNICIPAL_GIS`: Calibrated land-cover geometry.
  - `CENSUS_PROXY_DEMO`: Calibrated demographic distribution.

### Path to Ingesting Real City Datasets:
When deploying THERMOS for a live municipal partner:
1. **Raster Ingestion:** Replace thermal fields with zonal statistics extracted from Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs (COGs) of USGS Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 Surface Temperature products.
2. **Cadastral Polygons:** Ingest municipal ward boundaries via GeoJSON/Shapefile into the spatial repository.
3. **Census Ingestion:** Map block-group census tables directly to the `zone_demographics` schema.
4. **Zero Formula Changes:** The risk engine and simulation engine consume the standardized schemas unchanged.
